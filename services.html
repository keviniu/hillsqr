Frequently asked questions

What does Hillsqr do?

Hillsqr is an independent IT support and consulting business. It provides office IT support (desktops, laptops, Wi-Fi and networks, printers, Microsoft 365 and user accounts), IT consulting and solution architecture, web hosting and SEO improvement, software development and Agile/DevOps coaching.

Which areas does Hillsqr cover?

Small offices in Reading, Wokingham, Bracknell, Henley-on-Thames, Crowthorne and across the Thames Valley in Berkshire, UK. Support can be on site or remote.

What does office IT support include?

Desktop, laptop and peripheral support, network and Wi-Fi troubleshooting, printer support, Microsoft 365 and email, user accounts and access, application set-up and troubleshooting, and clear escalation to vendors or specialists when needed.

How much does IT support cost?

Every engagement starts with a clear scope, timeline and price before any work begins. Tell Hillsqr how many devices and servers you have and you will receive a straightforward proposal.

How quickly will Hillsqr reply to an enquiry?

Enquiries sent through the contact form or to info@hillsqr.com are answered within one working day. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 6:00pm.

Can Hillsqr help with Google visibility and advertising?

Yes. Hillsqr helps with Google Business Profile set-up, SEO improvement and review of Google advertising settings. See the case study on Google Local Services Ads for a roofing company.

Is support on site or remote?

Both. Hillsqr offers on-site and remote support, on contract or on call.
