Privacy notice

The Price Wise Group – virtual assistant for calls and WhatsApp

Who is responsible

The Price Wise Group (John) is responsible for the personal data you give when you call or message us. Our virtual assistant is run for us by Hill Square IT Consulting & Support, which handles the data only on our instructions.

What the assistant does

If we can't answer your call, or you message us on WhatsApp, an AI virtual assistant may reply. It tells you it is an assistant, answers simple questions and takes your message, which it passes to John.

What we collect

Your phone number, what you say or type (we keep a written transcript, not an audio recording) and any details you give us, such as your name, address, the work you need and a good time to call back.

Why we use it

To reply to you, arrange a quote or visit, and keep a record of your enquiry. We don't sell your details or use them for marketing.

Who else handles it

  • Twilio – phone calls and text messages
  • Meta (WhatsApp) – WhatsApp messages
  • Anthropic – the AI that understands the conversation
  • Brevo – sending John the summary by email
  • Microsoft Azure – hosting, in the UK

They process the data only to provide their service.

How long we keep it

Transcripts and logs are deleted after 90 days. A message may be kept longer in our own records if it leads to a quote or job.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data. Ask in the chat, or email info@hillsqr.com and we'll pass it to John. If you're unhappy with how your data is handled you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
