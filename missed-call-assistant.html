Service 01 | Never miss a call

24/7 Missed Call Assistant

Never miss a business opportunity. When you can't answer, someone does, and you get a clear summary to follow up.

How many customers have you lost because you couldn't answer the phone?

You're busy serving customers, in a meeting, or simply unavailable. Meanwhile, a potential new customer calls. They don't leave a voicemail – they just call your competitor.

What if someone could answer those calls for you, even when you're unavailable?

What the Missed Call Assistant does

Our 24/7 Missed Call Assistant automatically handles calls you can't answer, engages with callers, captures their enquiries and sends you a clear summary so you can follow up when convenient.

  • 24/7 availability. Never leave an unanswered call unattended.
  • Capture new opportunities. Engage potential customers when they need you.
  • Instant summaries. Receive call details and enquiry summaries for follow-up.
  • More time for your business. Focus on your work while we handle missed calls.

How it works

Step 1

A call goes unanswered

You're busy or unavailable and can't pick up.

Step 2

The assistant takes over

It answers and engages with the caller instead of leaving them on an unanswered line.

Step 3

The enquiry is captured

It collects who called and what they need.

Step 4

You get a clear summary

You follow up when it suits you, with the details to hand.

Agreed with you before it goes live

Every business takes calls differently, so we set the details up with you first:

  • what the assistant says and what it should not say;
  • what happens if a caller needs to speak to a person;
  • which details are captured and where your summaries are sent;
  • how callers are told they are speaking to an automated assistant, and how any call recording or notes are handled in line with UK data protection rules.

The assistant is not suitable for emergency calls.
