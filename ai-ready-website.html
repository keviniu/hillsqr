Service 03 | AI-ready website

Is your website ready for AI?

Your website may look great to visitors, but can AI understand what your business actually does?

A new way of searching

More people are using ChatGPT, Google AI and Microsoft Copilot to find businesses, compare services and get recommendations. Is your website ready for this new way of searching?

A modern website needs more than an attractive design. It should offer a simple, user-friendly experience while presenting clear, structured and meaningful information that search engines and AI systems can understand.

What Hillsqr does

We review and optimise your website to make its content easier for both people and AI-powered search systems to discover, understand and reference.

  • Clear information structure. Organise your services, expertise and business information logically.
  • AI-friendly content. Present useful, relevant information in a format that search engines and AI systems can interpret.
  • Structured data. Implement appropriate schema markup to help explain your business, services and content.
  • Search engine accessibility. Check crawling, indexing, sitemaps and technical SEO.
  • Local business visibility. Make your services and target locations easier to understand.

How it works

Step 1

Review

We look at how your website reads to people, search engines and AI systems.

Step 2

Prioritise

You get a plain-English list of what to fix first and why.

Step 3

Optimise

We make the agreed changes to structure, content, schema markup and technical SEO.

Step 4

Check

We test that search engines can reach and read the updated pages.

We use the same methods on our own website, including clear page structure, structured data, a sitemap and a plain-text summary file for AI systems.

No one can promise that a particular search engine or AI tool will mention or recommend a business. We make your website easier to find, understand and reference, and we explain what we have done.

Don't just build a website for people to visit. Build one that AI can understand and recommend.

Tell us your website address and we'll explain what we would look at first.
