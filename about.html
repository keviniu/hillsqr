About Hillsqr

An independent IT consultancy built on one idea: technology should serve the business, not the other way round.

Company overview

Hillsqr provides IT support and consulting to businesses that need dependable technology and a partner who understands how their people actually work.

We combine hands-on, user-facing support with senior-level technical expertise. That means the person fixing your desktop problem also understands the architecture, integration and delivery issues behind it.

The name Hillsqr (“Hill Square”) comes from the Chinese character for hill, 山, doubled: two hills standing together, a picture of steady, dependable support. It also reflects our approach of being a genuine IT value partner to the business teams we serve.

Our principles

  • Business first. Technology choices follow business outcomes.
  • Clear communication. Plain English, honest estimates, no surprises.
  • Reliability. We treat your downtime as seriously as you do.
  • Continuous improvement. Fix the problem, then fix the cause.
