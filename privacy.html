Privacy & cookies

What we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Hillsqr provides IT support and consulting. You can contact us at info@hillsqr.com about anything on this page.

Enquiries

If you use the enquiry form we collect your name, email address, company (optional), the service you're interested in and your message. We use them only to reply to you and to keep a record of the enquiry. The form is processed for us by Formspree, who deliver the message to our mailbox. We don't sell or share your details for marketing.

Cookies and the Google tag

This site uses a Google tag to measure how well our online advertising works, for example whether a visitor from a Google ad sent an enquiry. The tag loads on every visit, but it is set so that no advertising or measurement cookies are stored unless you click Accept on the cookie banner. If you click Reject, or ignore the banner, cookies stay off. Even then, the tag may send Google limited technical information, such as the page address and your IP address, without using cookies. We don't use other analytics or social media trackers.

The site remembers your cookie choice in your browser's local storage so we don't ask on every page.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep technical logs such as IP addresses to run the service.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you. Email us to do so. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
