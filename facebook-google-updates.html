One update, two platforms: Facebook + Google

Post once on Facebook and keep your Google Business Profile fresh too, without doubling your workload.

Already updating Facebook, but not Google?

Your Facebook followers see your latest news, offers and business updates. But what about potential customers searching for your services on Google?

Your Google Business Profile is equally important for local visibility. Keeping it updated doesn't have to mean doing the work twice.

What Hillsqr does

We make it easier to repurpose your Facebook updates for your Google Business Profile, helping you maintain an active presence on both platforms without doubling your workload.

  • One update, two platforms. Make your Facebook content work harder.
  • Save time. Reduce repetitive posting and manual work.
  • Improve local visibility. Keep your Google Business Profile fresh and relevant.
  • Stay connected. Reach both your social media audience and people searching for your services.

How it works

Step 1

Post on Facebook

Write your update as usual and add a simple tag to the ones you want on Google.

Step 2

We prepare it for Google

The tag is removed, the text is tidied to fit Google's limits, and your photo and a link to your website are added.

Step 3

It appears once

Each update is shared only once, so your Google profile never fills up with duplicates.

Agreed with you at set-up

  • whether updates are published to Google automatically or checked by you first;
  • which Facebook posts are shared and which tag you use;
  • the button and link shown on each Google update.

This service works through your own Facebook Page and Google Business Profile, and depends on the access those platforms allow. Where a platform does not allow automatic posting, we prepare the update so it can be added quickly by hand.

Two birds with one stone. One update, more opportunities.

Tell us about your Facebook Page and Google Business Profile and we'll show you how it would work for you.
